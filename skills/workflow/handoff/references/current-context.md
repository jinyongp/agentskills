# Current decisions and constraints

Read when persisting or forwarding corrections, rejections or changed decisions.

- Record a selected design or preference as the current choice, with rationale
  needed to preserve its scope. Remove superseded alternatives from active context;
  retain a separate decision history only when required.
- Keep durable safety, legal, compatibility and data constraints with their scope
  and reason. Preserve exact exclusions when needed to enforce them. A positive
  summary must not erase an accepted omission or permission boundary.
- Keep task-local conditions while applicable. Record their scope and expiry
  condition when known; unknown expiry is not permission to discard them.
- If a correction's durability or scope is unclear, retain it for the current task
  without inventing a permanent rule. Ask only when classification changes the next
  action or what must be persisted; continue independent work meanwhile.
- When a decision changes, locate dependent active plans, handoffs and instructions
  through relevant links or narrow searches. Update stale assumptions only in records
  authorized by the task; otherwise name the discrepancy for the next owner.
  Do not rewrite unrelated history or scan the repository to erase every mention.

Example: after choosing the existing local store, record that choice and its accepted
single-device scope. Drop proposals for alternate storage from the next handoff, but
retain a requirement that private data stays on the device. A temporary migration
restriction remains until its stated exit condition is confirmed.
