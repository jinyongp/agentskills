# Four-week trial of weekly equipment-request reviews

Approve a four-week trial in which nonurgent equipment requests are reviewed every Tuesday, with a decision or status update provided within two business days after review.

Currently, coordinators review nonurgent requests when time permits, so staff cannot predict when their requests will be considered. A regular review schedule would give staff a clear expectation for consideration and follow-up.

During the proposed trial:

- Review nonurgent equipment requests every Tuesday.
- Provide a decision or status update within two business days after each review.
- Preserve the existing same-day escalation to the on-duty coordinator for urgent replacements. Urgent requests must not wait for the weekly review.
- Keep individual approvals subject to budget and equipment availability. The response deadline does not promise equipment delivery within two business days.

This proposal seeks approval for the trial process. It does not propose changes to budgets or staffing.

No trial has run, and current response times have not been measured. Predictability is the intended benefit; time savings have not been demonstrated. Continuing the current approach is an alternative, but it would leave staff without a predictable review schedule.

At the end of four weeks, assess whether nonurgent requests received a decision or status update within the proposed deadline and whether urgent requests retained their existing same-day route. Use those results to decide whether to continue, adjust, or stop the process.

**Decision requested:** Approve the four-week trial.
